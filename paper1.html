The whole paper on one page

What the paper looks like

  • A mix of short answer, longer answer and high tariff questions.
  • Some longer questions test the quality of your extended writing (discuss, evaluate).
  • Questions can ask you to follow and correct algorithms and programs.
  • About half the marks are AO1 (knowledge), with the rest split between AO2 (apply) and AO3 (evaluate).

More on the assessment objectives →

How to revise for Paper 1

  • Learn key terms precisely – lots of marks need the exact word. Use flashcards.
  • Practise the methods in 1.4 (binary, floating point, Boolean algebra) with worked examples. No calculator!
  • Draw the processes – fetch-decode-execute, compilation – as flowcharts.
  • Plan “discuss” answers for 1.5 legal and ethical issues – see planning extended answers.

Exam technique: read the exam tips, then the page for your next grade.
