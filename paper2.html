Paper 2: Algorithms and programming

All the topics that start with 2. – computational thinking, problem solving and programming, and algorithms. Paper 2 also uses what you learn for Paper 1.

See the Paper 2 topics Revise 1.4.2 data structures

The exam

  • 2 hours 30 minutes
  • 140 marks – about 1 minute per mark
  • 40% of the A level
  • Written paper, no calculator
  • Answer all questions in Sections A and B

Two sections

Section A

Algorithms and problem solving

  • Writing algorithms and using computational methods.
  • Programming techniques and problem solving.
  • A mix of short and longer questions.

Section B

Scenario-based problem solving

  • Starts with a scenario; the questions that follow use it.
  • Mostly high tariff questions on algorithms and programming.
  • Read the scenario carefully and use its details in every answer.

Pseudocode: questions use OCR’s pseudocode style (appendix 5d of the specification). You can answer in any clear pseudocode or a programming language, as long as the meaning is clear.

What the paper looks like

  • The biggest share of marks is AO2 – applying knowledge to problems.
  • Expect to trace, complete, correct and write algorithms.
  • Some high tariff questions are marked on the quality of your written response.
  • Questions compare algorithms using Big O.

More on the assessment objectives →

How to revise for Paper 2

  • Practise doing, not reading. Trace algorithms with trace tables.
  • Write the standard algorithms from memory, then test them with edge cases.
  • Compare algorithms (Big O, time and space) in comparison tables.
  • Explain tricky ideas like recursion and abstraction out loud – teach it.
  • Mix topics in each session, including Paper 1 – interleaving.
