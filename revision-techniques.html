Revision techniques

13 techniques that work well for Computer Science. Each has a page explaining why it works, how to do it, and two examples from the course.

Each topic page suggests the best techniques for that topic – but you can use any technique that works for you.

The three things that work best

  1. Test yourself – don’t just reread.
  2. Space it out – revisit topics over weeks.
  3. Mix it up – combine pictures and words, and mix topics.

Watch out for “busy” revision

Some revision feels productive but doesn’t help much:

  • Rereading notes – it feels familiar, so you think you know it.
  • Highlighting – it’s easy to highlight everything.
  • Copying out notes neatly – no thinking needed.

If revision feels easy, ask yourself: am I making my brain work?
