Exam tips and improving your grade

OCR A Level Computer Science • H446

From D to C

Secure the basics. Most marks at this level come from knowing things precisely (AO1) and banking the marks that are always available.

The big idea

Turn “I sort of know it” into “I can say it exactly”. Precise key terms and secure methods are worth reliable marks on every paper.

What often holds a D grade back

  • Gaps in knowledge – some topics never revised.
  • Answers that are nearly right but use vague words instead of key terms.
  • Questions left blank.
  • Calculation questions lost through mistakes in the method.

Five things to focus on

1. Learn key terms exactly

Examiners look for the right term. “Volatile” gets a mark; “it forgets stuff” may not.

Make flashcards for every key term in a topic.

2. Fill your gaps first

Use the red/amber/green ratings on the course content page. Start with the reds.

3. Bank the calculation marks

Binary, hex, two’s complement and Boolean questions follow set methods. Practise until they are automatic.

4. Never leave a blank

A blank is always zero. Write the key facts you know about the topic – it may earn a mark.

5. Know what each standard algorithm does

Be able to say what bubble, insertion, merge and quick sort, and linear and binary search, do and when each is used.

Before and after

Example 1

Using precise key terms

Question: Describe two differences between RAM and ROM. [2]

D-grade answer Likely 1 mark

RAM is memory that is fast and ROM is read only memory.

C-grade answer 2 marks

RAM is volatile, so its contents are lost when the power is off, but ROM is non-volatile. RAM can be read from and written to, but ROM can only be read.

What made the difference:

  • Two separate differences for two marks.
  • Key terms: volatile, non-volatile.
  • Each point mentions both RAM and ROM.
Example 2

Following the method

Question: Convert −37 into 8-bit two’s complement binary. Show your working. [2]

D-grade answer 0 marks

10100101

This is sign and magnitude, not two’s complement – a very common mix-up.

C-grade answer 2 marks

+37 = 0010 0101
Flip: 1101 1010
Add 1: 1101 1011

Check: −128 + 64 + 16 + 8 + 2 + 1 = −37 ✔

What made the difference:

  • The correct method, written step by step.
  • Working shown, so a slip in one step can still earn a method mark.
  • Checked by converting back.

Best revision techniques for this step

  • Flashcards – learn key terms exactly
  • Worked examples – secure calculation methods
  • Blurting – find your knowledge gaps
  • Mnemonics – remember lists like ACID

These suit this step best, but use any technique that works for you.
