Exam tips and improving your grade

OCR A Level Computer Science • H446

From C to B

Explain and apply. At this level you know the facts – now you need to say why they matter and use them in the scenario (AO2).

The big idea

Every fact needs a reason. Link each point to the question with “because”, “so” or “which means”.

What often holds a C grade back

  • Answers that list facts but don’t explain them.
  • General answers that ignore the scenario in the question.
  • Small errors in trace tables and code: loop limits, array indexes.
  • Describing only one side in a “compare” question.

Five things to focus on

1. Point + reason

Use connectives: because, so, which means, therefore. A point with a reason often earns two marks.

2. Use the scenario

Underline the people, data and needs in the question. Mention them in your answer.

3. Compare properly

Write about both things in each point: “X is …, whereas Y is …”.

4. Trace accurately

Practise trace tables until you can follow loops, arrays and conditions without mistakes.

5. Mark your own answers

Use mark schemes. Look at the wording that earns marks and copy that style.

Before and after

Example 1

Applying knowledge to the scenario

Question: A delivery driver uses a laptop in their van all day. Explain why a solid state drive is more suitable than a magnetic hard drive for this laptop. [4]

C-grade answer Likely 2 marks

SSDs are faster, have no moving parts, are more reliable and use less power.

B-grade answer 4 marks

An SSD has no moving parts, so it will not be damaged by the vibration of the van or if the laptop is dropped. It also uses less power, which means the battery will last longer while the driver is out on deliveries.

What made the difference:

  • Two features, each with a reason.
  • Each reason is linked to the driver and the van.
  • Fewer points, but every point is fully developed.
Example 2

Finding and fixing an error

Question: This algorithm should add up all the numbers in the array numbers. Identify the error and write a corrected line. [2]

01 total = 0
02 for i = 1 to numbers.length
03     total = total + numbers[i]
04 next i
05 print(total)

C-grade answer Likely 1 mark

The loop on line 2 is wrong.

B-grade answer 2 marks

Line 2: arrays start at index 0, so numbers[0] is never added, and the loop goes to numbers.length, which is past the end of the array.

for i = 0 to numbers.length - 1

What made the difference:

  • Says exactly what is wrong and why.
  • Gives a corrected line of code.
  • Shows understanding of array indexes – tested often in Paper 2.

Best revision techniques for this step

  • Teach it – practise explaining why
  • Comparison tables – answer compare questions properly
  • Trace tables – follow and fix algorithms
  • Concept maps – build “because” links between ideas

These suit this step best, but use any technique that works for you.
