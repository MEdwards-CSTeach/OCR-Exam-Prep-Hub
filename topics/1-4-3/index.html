Question

Mixed quiz

Mixed quiz – marked for you

1. Which expression is a NOR gate? AO1

NOR is NOT OR: ¬(A ∨ B).

2. ¬(A ∧ B) is equivalent to… AO2

De Morgan’s: break the line, change the sign.

3. How many rows are in a truth table with 4 inputs? AO1

2⁴ = 16.

4. Which K-map group size is allowed? AO1

Only 1, 2, 4 or 8.

5. A full adder is made from… AO1

Half adder 1 adds A and B, half adder 2 adds the carry in, and an OR gate combines the carries.

6. What does a D-type flip-flop store? AO1

One bit. Eight of them sharing a clock make a byte-sized register.
