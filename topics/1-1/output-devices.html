Output devices

Choose the right device to present results or act on the world, and justify why it fits.

What is an output device?

Hardware that presents data from the computer, or acts on the world. Choose with the same method as input: Name, Use, Justify from the scenario.

Visual

Show it

  • Monitor, TV
  • Projector
  • E-ink
  • LED lights

Hard copy

Keep it

  • Inkjet, laser
  • Thermal
  • 3D printer
  • Braille embosser

Sound

Hear it

  • Speakers
  • Headphones
  • Buzzer, alarm

Movement

Do it

  • Actuator: motor, valve, pump
  • Vibration (haptic)
  • Braille display

Both input and output: touchscreen, VR headset, a games controller that vibrates. Choose using: who sees or hears it, where (sunlight, noise, privacy), whether a hard copy is needed, and how much (volume, running cost, battery).

Actuators and control systems

  • An actuator turns a signal from the computer into movement: a motor, valve or pump.
  • Examples: central heating (valve opens, pump turns on), automatic doors (motor slides the door).

Watch out for:

  • Giving an input device when an output is asked for.
  • A device that doesn’t fit: a printer for video editing.
  • “To output data” as a reason – that is true of every device.
  • Describing how a printer works instead of when to use it.

Quick quiz – marked for you

1. A solar-powered bus stop sign. AO2

E-ink only uses power when the image changes, so a small solar panel is enough. It is readable in sunlight.

2. Receipts at a shop till. AO2

Thermal printers are fast, quiet and small, with no ink to replace.

3. Which is output only? AO1

A touchscreen and VR headset are both input and output. A microphone is input.

4. An office prints 2,000 pages a day. AO2

A laser printer is fast with a low cost per page for high volumes.

5. What is an actuator? AO1

Actuators (motors, valves, pumps) act on the world, often controlled by readings from a sensor.

Exam-style questions – mark them yourself

Write your answer, then open Mark your answer. Tick the mark points you hit, read the model answer and use the tips to improve. See the 1.1 exam tips for more help.

AO1[2 marks]

A user captures and edits video elements before combining them into a final video. State two different output devices that could be used when editing the videos. (June 2023)

Mark your answer

Tick each mark point your answer includes.

Model answer: A monitor, to view the video while editing, and speakers or headphones, to hear the sound.

How to improve a weak answer:

  • They must be output devices, and suitable for video editing – a printer is not credited.
AO1[2 marks]

Explain what is meant by an actuator. Give an example of its use.

Mark your answer

Tick each mark point your answer includes.

Model answer: An actuator turns a signal from the computer into physical movement. For example, a motor that opens a greenhouse window when a temperature sensor shows it is too hot.

How to improve a weak answer:

  • Say what it does (movement) and give a specific example.
AO3[4 marks]

A delivery depot prints two-part delivery notes in a dusty loading bay, and colour brochures for customers in the office. Recommend a printer for each and justify your choices.

Mark your answer

Tick each mark point your answer includes.

Model answer: For the delivery notes, a dot matrix printer, because it prints through to the second copy and copes with the dusty loading bay. For the brochures, a colour laser printer, because it is fast with a low cost per page when printing large numbers.

How to improve a weak answer:

  • Printer (1) + reason (1), twice.
  • Use the scenario’s details: two-part, dusty, colour, many copies.
  • Accept inkjet for brochures if few are printed and photo quality matters.

How to revise this

  • Comparison tables – printers and screens: best for, strength, drawback
  • Flowcharts and process diagrams – draw a sensor–processor–actuator control system
  • Flashcards – scenario on the front, the best output device on the back

These are the best fit for this part of the topic, but you can use any revision technique that works for you.
